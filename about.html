Professional biography

About me

I am an accounting student from Phoenix who wants to help people get their finances in order. I am also the treasurer of my chapter, so I already work with other people's money.

Where I am from and what I study

I am from Phoenix, Arizona. I study accounting with a minor in financial planning at the University of Arizona's Eller College of Management. I expect to graduate in May 2028. My cumulative GPA is 3.968, and I made the Dean's List in Spring 2025, Fall 2025 and Spring 2026.

What I do with money today

I am the treasurer of the University of Arizona Delta Chi chapter. My work covers financial oversight, contract negotiation, vendor management, dues collection and compliance. Dues are the fees members pay to fund the chapter.

I handle this money for my brothers, so trust matters in every decision. You can see the specific systems I built on my portfolio page.

What I have seen inside an advisory firm

In summer 2026 I completed a mini internship shadowing the founder of an Arizona RIA. An RIA, or registered investment advisor, is a firm registered to give investment advice. It is legally required to put its clients' interests first. I describe what I observed and helped with in my portfolio.

Why financial planning

I want to help people get their finances in order so they have the freedom to live the life they want and take care of the people close to them.

That is why I plan to pursue the CFP (Certified Financial Planner) credential right after I graduate. The CFP is a professional certification for financial planners. I do not hold it yet.
