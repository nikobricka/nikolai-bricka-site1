Chapter treasury system

Context

I am the treasurer of the University of Arizona Delta Chi chapter. The role covers about $350K in annual chapter budget and about $250K in annual dues. Dues are the fees members pay to fund the chapter.

My role

This was a solo project. I led every part of it.

Skills

  • Budgeting
  • Fee design
  • Policy writing
  • Platform migration
  • Cash management
  • Collections

STAR

Situation
The chapter runs on about $350K in annual budget, funded by about $250K in annual dues. I am responsible for both.
Task
Collect dues, control spending and keep the chapter’s cash working.
Action
  • Led the migration of the chapter’s payments from its previous platform to LegFi, a platform that collects member payments and tracks invoices.
  • Designed tiered dues with grandfathering. Tiered means dues are set at different levels. Grandfathering means existing members keep the terms they already had when a new structure starts.
  • Wrote a budget accountability policy that ties dues adjustments to the spending of budget holders, the members who spend from the chapter budget.
  • Opened a savings account for idle funds.
Result
93% dues collection on about $250K in annual dues. The collection rate is the share of billed dues that members pay.

Files and images

Why it matters

A wealth firm bills for its services, tracks what it collects and puts idle cash to work. This project shows I can build that process and hold people accountable for spending.

Contract review control and contract analyzer app

Context

A chapter contract was signed without treasurer review. Venue, catering, DJ and transportation contracts carry payment terms and cancellation fees that affect the chapter’s budget.

My role

This was a solo project. I designed the process and built the app myself.

Skills

  • Process design
  • Contract risk spotting
  • Web development
  • Plain-English communication

STAR

Situation
A chapter contract was signed without treasurer review.
Task
Make sure the treasurer reviews every contract before it is executed.
Action
  • Created a formal process that requires treasurer approval before any contract is executed.
  • Built a contract analyzer app with two tools. React is a JavaScript tool for building web apps. The Anthropic API is a service that lets software use the Claude AI model. The app reads venue, catering, DJ and transportation contracts. It flags payment terms, cancellation fees and red flags in plain English.
Result
The analyzer has helped with negotiation and with contract edit requests.

Files and images

Why it matters

Advisors read fee schedules and cancellation terms on behalf of clients. Checking terms before money moves is basic client protection, and I built a control for it.
